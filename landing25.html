BigPay Application

BigPay is an all-in-one payment management app for sellers and merchants, built with Flutter on top of a Java Spring Boot backend. It puts real-time sales insights, transaction management, invoice tracking, payouts, and holds in the palm of your hand, backed by push notifications, two-factor authentication, and full Arabic and English support. A business command center built to save time and give financial clarity.

Key features

What the app does

Real-Time Sales Insights

Gross and net sales at a glance, with pending, successful, failed, refunded, and disputed transactions across custom date ranges.

Transaction Management

Search, filter, and review every transaction with card holder, store, and payment status details. Issue refunds in seconds.

Invoice Tracking

Detailed breakdowns of subtotals, taxes, fees, and totals, with status tracking from creation to payment and one-tap cancellation.

Payout Monitoring

Track payout schedules and methods, monitor holds and released amounts, and review full payout summaries.

Holds Management

Full transparency over funds on hold with a breakdown of active and released holds and your available balance.

Instant Alerts

Real-time push notifications for transactions, invoices, and account activity.

Secure & Reliable

Two-factor authentication, secure token storage, and encrypted session management.

Built for Business

Multi-seller support, card network volume breakdown, sales trend charts, and a clean bilingual interface built for speed.
