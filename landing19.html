Flikk Application

Flikk is a sports app where you find on-demand workouts based on how much time you have. It suggests carefully selected classes with trusted instructors, and loyalty points let you join sessions for free. Users can request to upgrade from trainee to trainer or gym account, pay online, and switch between dark and light themes.
